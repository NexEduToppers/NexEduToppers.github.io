1. Chapter Introduction | अध्याय का परिचय

The chapter “Fit Body, Fit Mind, Fit Nation” highlights the importance of physical activity, mental well-being, music, sports and learning from mistakes.

The poem describes children who enjoy different activities. Anant plays hockey, Geeta practises skating, Lhamo plays a guitar, Shabana participates in a cycle race, and Meena enjoys singing.

Main idea: A healthy body, a positive mind and active participation in life help children grow and learn.

Learning objectives

  • Understand the poem and identify the children's activities.
  • Learn important vocabulary and rhyming words.
  • Recognise naming words and describing words.
  • Practise spelling words with double letters.
  • Talk about sports, music, health and personal interests.

2. Chapter Summary and Poem Explanation | सारांश

The poem introduces children with different interests and talents. Each child enjoys an activity and develops skills through practice.

Children and their activities

ChildQuality or interestActivity
AnantActivePlays hockey
GeetaGracefulPractises skating and learns from mistakes
LhamoProudPlays the guitar
Lhamo's friendMusical interestPlays the sitar
ShabanaSwiftWins a cycle race
MeenaMusicalSings in rhythm

Understanding the message

  • Sports: Playing games gives us opportunities to move, practise and develop skills.
  • Learning from mistakes: Mistakes help us understand what we need to improve.
  • Music and rhythm: Music can be an enjoyable way to express ourselves.
  • Different talents: Every child may have different interests and abilities.
  • Participation: Enjoying activities and practising regularly can help us improve.
Exam tip: Remember each child's name and activity. Questions may ask who plays hockey, who practises skating, who plays the guitar or sitar, who wins a cycle race, and who sings in rhythm.

4. Grammar Corner | व्याकरण

A. Naming words (Nouns)

A noun is a naming word. It names a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples: Anant, Geeta, guitar, hockey, nation, children.

B. Describing words (Adjectives)

An adjective tells us more about a noun. It can describe its colour, size, quality or condition.

Describing wordNaming wordPhrase
BlueSkyBlue sky
HardworkingChildrenHardworking children
GreenLeavesGreen leaves
PebbledRoadPebbled road
HealthyVegetablesHealthy vegetables

C. Add describing words

Examples of adjectives that can describe these nouns:

  • Mangoes: sweet, ripe, yellow
  • Baby: cheerful, little, cute
  • Nation: great, strong, peaceful
  • Winner: happy, proud, deserving

More than one adjective may correctly describe a noun.

D. Double-letter words

Some English words contain two identical letters next to each other. These are often called double-letter words.

  • Village — ll
  • Happy — pp
  • Little — tt
  • Skating — no double letter
  • Cheerful — ee
  • Swimming — mm
  • Class — ss
  • Book — oo

E. Rhyming words

Rhyming words have the same or similar ending sounds. The poem uses pairs such as these:

  • Hockey — rocky
  • Skates — mistakes
  • Guitar — sitar
  • Race — pace
  • Joy — boy
Remember: Rhyming depends on the sound at the end of a word, not simply on its spelling.

5. Important Question–Answers | प्रश्न-उत्तर

Textbook questions

Q1. Which game does Anant play?

Answer: Anant plays hockey.

Q2. Which musical instruments are mentioned in the poem?

Answer: The guitar and the sitar are mentioned in the poem.

Q3. Who among the children mentioned in the poem likes moving in rhythm?

Answer: Meena enjoys singing in rhythm.

Q4. Who learns every day from the mistakes made during skating?

Answer: Geeta learns from her mistakes while practising skating.

Additional exam-practice questions

Q5. What does the poem tell us about children's interests?

Answer: The poem shows that children have different interests, such as sports, skating, cycling, playing instruments and singing.

Q6. Why is it useful to learn from mistakes?

Answer: Mistakes help us recognise what went wrong and improve through practice.

Q7. What is an adjective? Give two examples.

Answer: An adjective is a word that describes a noun. Examples: blue sky and cheerful children.

Q8. What is the central message of the poem?

Answer: The poem celebrates different talents and the joy of participating in sports, music and other activities.

Q9. What are rhyming words? Give an example from the poem.

Answer: Rhyming words have similar ending sounds. “Race” and “pace” are rhyming words.

Q10. What are the Paralympic Games?

Answer: The Paralympic Games are international sports competitions for athletes with disabilities.

6. Speaking and Real-Life Practice | बोलने का अभ्यास

Ask the child to answer these questions aloud in complete sentences.

  1. Which game do you usually play at school?
  2. Do you prefer indoor games or outdoor games? Why?
  3. Which musical instrument would you like to learn?
  4. Which dance form do you like most?
  5. What healthy habit do you follow every day?
  6. What do you do when you make a mistake?
Speaking pattern:

I enjoy playing ______ because ______.

I would like to learn ______.

When I make a mistake, I ______.

To stay healthy, I ______ every day.

7. Creative Activities | रचनात्मक गतिविधियाँ

A. Describe a noun

Write two describing words for each naming word.

B. Find double-letter words

Write five words with two identical letters next to each other.

C. Rhyming words

Write a word that rhymes with each word below.

Describing words: sweet mangoes, little baby, strong nation, proud winner.

Double-letter words: happy, little, book, class, swimming.

Rhymes: rocky, mistakes, sitar, pace, boy.

Other correct answers may also be possible.

D. Preparing a dish without fire

With adult supervision, prepare a simple dish that does not require cooking, such as a fruit salad. Wash fruit carefully and ask an adult to help with cutting. Describe the ingredients and steps to your class.

E. Explore the Paralympic Games

Find out about the Paralympic Games and write three facts about the athletes, sports or events. Explain how sports can encourage participation and achievement.

8. Think & Try Yourself | स्वयं सोचें और उत्तर दें

Try answering before looking at the sample answers.

1. Write one positive quality that starts with the same letter as your name.

2. What do you enjoy more: an indoor game or an outdoor game? Explain why.

3. Name one musical instrument you would like to learn.

4. How can you keep your body and mind healthy?

5. What should you do when you make a mistake?

1. Example: Anant — active.

2. Example: I prefer outdoor games because they let me move and play in open spaces.

3. Example: I would like to learn the guitar.

4. Example: I can exercise, eat balanced meals, sleep well and relax.

5. Example: I should understand my mistake, learn from it and try again.

These are examples. Personal answers may be different.
