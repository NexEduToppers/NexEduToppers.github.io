1. Chapter Notes | अध्याय के नोट्स

Story in Simple Words

Chinna wants to buy tinkling bells for Tara, his young goat. At Chacha's shop, he discovers that his money is missing. He feels sad and tells his mother, Kamala. She comforts him and takes him to the market.

At the fruit stall, the seller accidentally gives Chinna ₹20 as change when the correct change is ₹10. Chinna remembers how sad he felt when his own money went missing. He returns the extra ₹10 to the seller. The seller appreciates his honesty. Chinna later buys the bells and puts them around Tara's neck.

चिन्ना अपनी बकरी तारा के लिए घंटियाँ खरीदना चाहता है। उसे पता चलता है कि उसके पैसे गुम हो गए हैं। बाजार में फल वाला गलती से उसे ₹10 अधिक लौटा देता है। चिन्ना अतिरिक्त पैसे वापस कर देता है। इससे उसकी ईमानदारी दिखाई देती है। बाद में वह घंटियाँ खरीदकर तारा के गले में बाँध देता है।

Important Characters

  • Chinna: A thoughtful and honest boy.
  • Kamala: Chinna's caring mother.
  • Tara: Chinna's young goat.
  • Fruit seller: The person who gives extra change by mistake.
  • Chacha: The shopkeeper who sells the bells.

Story Sequence

  1. Chinna sees the bells he wants for Tara.
  2. He discovers that his money is missing.
  3. He tells his mother, and she comforts him.
  4. He goes to the market with his mother.
  5. He returns the extra ₹10 to the fruit seller.
  6. He buys the bells and puts them around Tara's neck.

Comprehension Questions

  1. Why was Chinna upset at Chacha's shop?
  2. How did Kamala help Chinna?
  3. What mistake did the fruit seller make?
  4. Why did Chinna return the extra money?
  5. What did Chinna do after buying the bells?
Show Sample Answers | उत्तर देखें
  1. His money was missing, so he could not buy the bells.
  2. She comforted him and took him to the market.
  3. He gave Chinna ₹20 instead of the correct change of ₹10.
  4. Chinna knew that the extra money did not belong to him.
  5. He put the bells around Tara's neck.

2. Important Vocabulary | महत्वपूर्ण शब्द

Tinkling — a light ringing sound Missing — lost or not found Comfort — help someone feel better Honest — truthful and fair Careful — taking care to avoid mistakes Praise — say something good about someone Change — money returned after payment Seller — a person who sells things

Tinkling — छन-छन की आवाज़; missing — गुम; comfort — सांत्वना देना; honest — ईमानदार; careful — सावधान; praise — प्रशंसा; change — लौटाए गए पैसे; seller — विक्रेता।

3. The Lesson of the Story | कहानी की सीख

Honesty means doing what is right, even when nobody is forcing us to do it. Chinna returns the extra money because it belongs to the seller.

ईमानदारी का अर्थ है सही काम करना, चाहे कोई हमें देख रहा हो या नहीं। अतिरिक्त पैसे लौटाकर छिन्ना ईमानदारी दिखाता है।

Think about it: If someone gives you too much change by mistake, tell them politely and return the extra amount.
सोचें: यदि कोई गलती से अधिक पैसे दे दे, तो विनम्रता से बताकर अतिरिक्त पैसे लौटा दें।

4. Grammar: Comparing Things | तुलना करना

Comparative adjectives compare two people, animals, or things. Many short adjectives use -er + than. Superlative adjectives compare one member with a whole group and often use the + -est.

Use Example Meaning
Comparative Tara is smaller than a horse. Compares two things
Comparative This bag is lighter than that bag. Compares two things
Superlative Ravi is the fastest runner in the group. Compares with a group
Superlative January is the coldest month here. Shows the highest degree in a group

दो चीज़ों की तुलना में अक्सर adjective + -er than आता है। किसी समूह में सबसे अधिक गुण बताने के लिए अक्सर the + adjective-est का प्रयोग होता है।

5. Speaking and Real-Life Practice | बोलने का अभ्यास

Discuss what you would do in each situation. Give a reason for your answer.

  1. You accidentally break a pen that you borrowed. What should you do?
  2. Your friends ask you to cheat during a quiz. What will you say?
  3. A classmate takes your notebook without asking. How will you respond politely?
  4. Your classmate has no snack. How could you help?

हर परिस्थिति में बताइए कि आप क्या करेंगे और क्यों। ईमानदारी, विनम्रता और दूसरों की मदद करने के बारे में सोचें।
