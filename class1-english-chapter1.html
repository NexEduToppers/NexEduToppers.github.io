Welcome to Chapter 1 - Two Little Hands

What We Learn

This chapter helps children learn about different parts of the body and what we use them for. Like

  • Hands help us clap, hold and do many activities.
  • Legs help us walk, run and move.
  • Eyes help us see.
  • Ears help us hear.
  • Nose helps us smell.
  • Mouth helps us eat and speak.
  • Head is an important part of our body.
